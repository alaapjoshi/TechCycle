Upload flow

One photo, one listing

The submit handler uploads the photo first and only writes the listing once Storage returns a download URL. A failed upload never creates a listing.

1

Pick a photo

Expo Image Picker keeps the selection on the device until the seller taps Add Item.

2

Upload to Firebase Storage

The image is stored under Pictures/ and the app waits for its download URL.

3

Write the Firestore listing

Title, notes, price, condition, and the image URL are saved to the foryou collection.
